Sproutwell privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Sproutwell: Cozy Pipe Puzzle does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • To get new weekly levels, the app downloads level files from this website (GitHub Pages). Like any website, GitHub's servers may briefly log the request; we do not receive or use that data.
